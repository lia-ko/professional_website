The blueprint — Day Edition
01 · Foundations

How I build

I'm a little obsessed with the craft of it. Good software is maintainable software — designed for the change that's coming, not just the ticket in front of me. I lean on SOLID and DRY, clear separation of concerns, and code that's tested and documented so the next person (usually future me) isn't left digging. I build systems meant to be extended and to evolve, not torn down and rebuilt a year later.
